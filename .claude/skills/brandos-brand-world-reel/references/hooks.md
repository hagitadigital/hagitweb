# Hooks — the first 2.8 seconds

A hook is one line plus one visual proof. Its only job is to make the viewer stop and recognise themself.
The rest of the reel is the answer; the outro closes the loop by answering the hook out loud.

## Contents
1. Studio mode — the client's pain (default today)
2. Brand mode — the brand's own hook (for BrandOS)
3. Writing rules for both
4. How to pick the hook for a world

---

## 1. Studio mode — the client's pain

The viewer is a business owner (beauty, cosmetics, aesthetics, boutique food, jewellery, fragrance, cafés).
The hook names the pain that makes her look for a studio. The world that follows proves that a brand built
from one promise escapes that pain. Speak to "you" (feminine singular, as on the site), never to "business owners".

| Pain | Hook line | Visual proof in the hook | Outro answer (closes the loop) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compared on price | למה תמיד משווים אותך במחיר? | Grey product card, price, "ככה משווים" tag (price-card mechanic) | "את BRAND° לא משווים. בוחרים." · "ואת העסק שלך?" |
| Not recognisable | אם נסתיר את הלוגו, עדיין יזהו אותך? | The world's product with a copper bar sliding over the logo (hidden-logo mechanic) | "את BRAND° מזהים גם בלי הלוגו." · "ואת העסק שלך?" |
| Interchangeable | אם המוצר שלך היה עומד ליד המתחרה, איך היו יודעים שהוא שלך? | Two grey near-identical cards side by side | "כי כל פרט יוצא מהבטחה אחת." |
| "Too expensive" | למה אצלה זה "יקר", ואצלך שואלים אם יש הנחה? | Grey card with a discount sticker | "יקר זה מחיר בלי סיפור." |
| Scroll-past | הלקוחה גוללת. מה יעצור אותה דווקא אצלך? | Fast grey scroll of generic product shots that freezes | "עולם שרוצים להיכנס אליו, ולהישאר." |
| Good product, weak pull | יש לך מוצר מצוין. אז למה בוחרים במתחרה? | Grey card with ★★★★★ and an empty cart | "כי הבחירה לא מתחילה במוצר." |

Pair the pain with the world's strongest proof:
- Hidden-logo hook → worlds with a signature that is not the logo (ROSÉE's dial and drop, ELVÉA's leaf line, CREMA's colour-per-capsule, ENCORE's sundial, MIEL's slice).
- Price hook → worlds whose product is a commodity on a shelf (serums, cakes, coffee capsules).
- "Too expensive" → luxury worlds (VELUNE, ENCORE, AURELLE).

The campaign line family that already ran (keep the voice consistent with it):
"למה מוצר אחד משווים, ואת השני פשוט בוחרים?" · "זה לא עיצוב מחדש. זה שינוי הדרך שבה העסק שלך נתפס." · "אל תהיי המוצר שמשווים. בני את המותג שבוחרים."

## 2. Brand mode — the brand's own hook (BrandOS)

The reel is published by the brand itself, to its own customers. No studio window, no HUD, no "עולם מותג N",
no "אבחון פער המותג". The hook comes from the brand's Brand DNA:

| Brand DNA field | Becomes |
|---|---|
| audience pain / moment | the hook line (a question or a tension, in the brand's tone) |
| promise | the outro line that answers it |
| signature element (not the logo) | the opening image under the hook |
| CTA (shop / book / visit) | the last frame |

Formula: **[the customer's moment] → [what the brand promises about it]**, shown on the signature element.

Examples written from the worlds already built:
- ROSÉE° — hook "העור שלך מתעורר לפנייך?" over the falling drop · outro "טיפה אחת, כל בוקר."
- ELVÉA° — hook "טבע או מדע? למה לבחור." over the flower and the leaf · outro "הטבע מתחיל את הסיפור. הפורמולה משלימה אותו."
- CREMA° — hook "הבוקר שלך מתחיל בהחלטה." over the capsules turning · outro "הבוקר שלך, בגוון שלך."
- MIEL° — hook "מה בפנים?" over the knife going into the cake · outro "רומנטיקה מתחילה מבפנים."
- ENCORE° — hook "איזו שעה את?" over the sundial lid turning · outro "תכשיט אחד. שנים עלייך."

In brand mode use the brand's palette and fonts for the hook frame too, and replace the studio intro with a
1.5s logo reveal on the brand's background. Timeline: hook 0–2.5 · world 2.5–27 · logo + CTA 27–30.

## 3. Writing rules (both modes)

- One line, at most ~9 words, Hebrew first. A question or a tension, never a slogan.
- Concrete over abstract: "במחיר", "הלוגו", "הנחה" beat "ערך", "זהות", "בידול".
- The hook must be answerable by what the reel shows. If the world cannot prove it, pick another hook.
- Copy comes from the world's own site where possible; quote it, don't paraphrase it.
- Avoid stacking "לא X, אלא Y" constructions; one per reel at most (the campaign already owns one).
- Say "עולם מותג" — never "Monde" — in Hebrew copy.
- Feminine singular address ("את", "שלך") for the studio audience, as on hagitantebi.co.il.

## 4. Picking the hook for a world

Offer three hooks in the spec (two pains + one alternative), each with its proof mechanic and its outro answer,
and mark one as recommended with a one-line reason (which signature element proves it best).
The owner picks; never build before the hook is approved.
