# RUE° mini ad · render notes
- `RUE-MINI-9x16.mp4` 1080x1920, 15.5s, 30fps; `RUE-MINI-4x5.mp4` 1080x1350 (reel whole in the centre, cream side panels). No audio.
- Same character and format as the dessert and beauty mini reels (branch `claude/tender-cannon-lf0opi`).
- Scenes: hook (her walking clip on the copper line, cream ground, the site's hero lighting up under the line) · the scale model · the plate "14" · the key and the lobby · CTA (she sits on the button).
- `character/scene-*.jpg`: stills generated by Hagit (GPT Image 2.6, from the character sheet plus RUE°'s renders).
- `character/clips/model.mp4`, `plate.mp4`, `key.mp4`: Kling `kling-video-v3_0_turbo`, 5s, 1080p, from those stills; audio removed. `walk.mp4` is the existing walk clip from the beauty mini reel.
- Source: `src/gen.py` writes `src/reel.html`; render with the brandos-brand-world-reel skill's `scripts/render.js src/reel.html 15.5 <out.mp4>`.
- Clip frames are not in the repo. Rebuild them from `character/clips/`:
  - `ffmpeg -i character/clips/walk.mp4 -vf "colorchannelmixer=rr=1.0082:gg=1.0087:bb=1.0167,crop=720:800:0:0,scale=1080:1200:flags=lanczos" -q:v 2 src/assets/walk/f%03d.jpg`
  - `ffmpeg -i character/clips/<name>.mp4 -vf "select='gte(n\,S)',setpts=N/FRAME_RATE/TB,scale=1080:1920:flags=lanczos" -frames:v 80 -q:v 3 src/assets/<name>/f%03d.jpg` with S = 8 (model), 6 (plate), 10 (key)
- 4:5: `src/frame-4x5.png` over a `#F2EBDC` ground (ffmpeg scale 760:1350, overlay 160:0).
